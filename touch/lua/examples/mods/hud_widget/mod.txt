# A HUD panel with settings the Mods tab shows (docs/PLUGIN_STANDARD.md).
id: hud_widget
title: Health and armor bars
version: 1.0
author: Godnotic
homepage: https://github.com/dixonSolutions/Godnotic/tree/main/touch/lua/examples/mods/hud_widget
description: Two bars for your health and armor, placed clear of the touch controls.
api: 2
realm: client
permissions: events, display, players
reason.events: draws the bars on every HUD frame (the hud event)
reason.display: draws the two bars
reason.players: reads your own health and armor (xt.me)
setting.side: choice left left right | Screen edge
setting.width: number 160 80 320 20 | Bar width
setting.numbers: bool 1 | Show the numbers
