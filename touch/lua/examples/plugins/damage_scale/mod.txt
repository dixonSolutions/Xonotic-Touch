# A server plugin that changes the game through a write-capable hook.
id: damage_scale
title: Damage scale
version: 1.0
author: Godnotic
description: Scales all damage between players (sv_lua_damage_scale_factor) and counts what it changed.
api: 2
realm: server
permissions: hooks, hooks_write, messages
reason.hooks: sees every hit through the Damage_Calculate hook
reason.hooks_write: changes the damage of a hit
reason.messages: tells a player once how much their hits are scaled
setting.factor: number 0.5 0 4 0.25 | Damage factor (1 is normal)
setting.selfdamage: bool 0 | Scale self damage too
